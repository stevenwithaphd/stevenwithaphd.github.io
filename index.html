The Intersection of Theory and Practice

Welcome to the primary site of Steven R. Walton, Ph.D., CISSP.

As a Senior Cybersecurity Manager, I lead a team dedicated to defending the critical network infrastructure of research and education institutions across the state. My operational focus is grounded in strategic risk management, threat mitigation, and the realities of defending SLED environments operating under strict resource constraints.

I recently completed my doctoral research, which investigates the Cyber Hygiene approaches of K-12 technology directors. By leveraging Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis and the Health Belief Model, my work deconstructs the human-centric variables that dictate why systemic vulnerabilities persist in educational networks despite established security protocols.

This domain serves as a centralized repository for:

  • Academic Insights: Bridging the gap between the Health Belief Model and practical IT administration.
  • Operational Frameworks: Translating high-level risk management theory into tactical deployments for small technical teams.
  • Industry Analysis: Observations on the evolving threat landscape facing the EdTech sector.
Further documentation, publications, and strategic guidance will be deployed sequentially.
